## Cameras & angles

A PTZ camera is a camera that can be controlled remotely to point & zoom in a chosen direction. PTZ stands for pan, tilt & zoom. They are often more expensive than static cameras of the same quality.

For enclosure main cameras, being able to reframe and control the view of the camera is key to keep the stream interesting, focused on the animals and makes the install location of the camera less important. This view can be controlled by staff, trusted operators or the broader community.

This is especially true for large enclosures with active animals, which may need several PTZ cameras to cover the whole enclosure.

Even for animals that enjoy their favourite spot and perch very contently like World Bird Sanctuary's Sayyida, which would be served by a static camera for 98% of the stream, a PTZ camera captures moments that might be missed otherwise.
