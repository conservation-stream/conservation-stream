## Cameras & angles

There are broadly three kinds of cameras you can use to stream live cams:

* A static or bullet camera - This is a non-moving camera with a fixed angle that is set when it's installed.
* A PTZ camera - This is a camera that can be controlled remotely to point & zoom. They are often more expensive than static cameras of the same quality.
* A phone, computer or portable camera - While not suited for permanent installations, a device with a camera & WiFi is a great way to get streams up and running quickly.

For enclosure main cameras, being able to reframe and control the view of the camera is key to keep the stream interesting, focused on the animals and makes the install location of the camera less important. This view can be controlled by staff, trusted operators or the broader community.

This is especially true for large enclosures with active animals, which may need several PTZ cameras to cover the whole enclosure. This is also where a broader group of camera operators who can control what the camera is looking at can really bring the stream to life. Read here to learn more about community involvement in controlling cams.
