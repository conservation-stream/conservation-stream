A PTZ camera is a camera that can be controlled remotely to point & zoom in a chosen direction. PTZ stands for pan, tilt & zoom. They are often more expensive than static cameras of the same quality.

For enclosure main cameras, being able to reframe and control the view of the camera is key to keep the stream interesting, focused on the animals and makes the install location of the camera less important. This view can be controlled by staff, trusted operators or the broader community.  This is especially true for large outside enclosures which may need several PTZ cameras to cover the whole enclosure.

It's also really nice to have alternate angles that focus on interesting specific behaviours: favourite basking spots, food and water bowls, hides or dens. These detail shots often become moments that people remember.
