We're a collaborative network of organizations, communities, and individuals who believe 24/7 live cameras and streaming are powerful conservation tools. This non-commercial, open-source project shares knowledge and resources for creating conservation-focused live streams.

Live cameras offer a different, intimate way to build relationships with animals than a typical in-person visit. Viewers watch while they work, eat, study and rest. A live stream moves at the animal's natural pace - gentle, slow and entirely their own. Left open on a second monitor or a TV, it lets a quiet connection grow little by little.

We believe that 24/7 livecams are positive contributors to animal care in lots of special ways. That said, there are some concerns that are raised by the inherent public exposure that comes with them. This guide covers those concerns and outlines some strategies to mitigate that risk.

This guide is for organizations exploring conservation-focused live streams. It covers where live cameras may work well for conservation storytelling, how they may support animal care, how camera control and privacy can be managed, and how community interaction can be handled responsibly.

Live streams are one way to tell conservation stories, not a replacement for in-person visits. In some cases, they can help organizations share animals and conservation work that would be difficult or unsuitable to present in a public visitor setting.
